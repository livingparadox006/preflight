# ENE 310 — Lab 5: Heat Exchanger Performance

Submit a **7-page technical report** as a single PDF by **Thursday at 16:00**.

The report must include a labelled schematic of the shell-and-tube rig, a table of inlet and outlet temperatures for both streams, and a plot of effectiveness against flow rate.

Use at least **six flow rates** between 0.5 and 3.0 L/min.
Calculate the log-mean temperature difference for every run and state the units.
Compare the measured effectiveness with the counter-flow prediction.
Discuss at least **two instrumental sources of error** and one procedural source.
The conclusion must state whether the rig meets the manufacturer's stated effectiveness of 0.72.
Figures must be numbered, captioned, and referenced in the text.
Submit the spreadsheet as an appendix; do not paste raw code into the report.
