# Heat Exchanger Lab — Report

## 1. Introduction

This report investigates the performance of a shell-and-tube heat exchanger. The rig was operated at several flow rates and the temperatures were recorded. The results were generally good and the equipment worked as expected.

## 2. Method

The hot stream was set to a constant inlet temperature. The cold stream flow rate was changed between runs. Temperatures were read from the display after the system settled. A schematic is shown in Figure 1.

## 3. Results

The effectiveness increased as the cold flow rate increased. The measured values were close to the prediction. The log-mean temperature difference was calculated for each run. A spreadsheet is attached.

## 4. Discussion

The main errors were probably due to the equipment and the way the experiment was run. Some heat may have been lost to the surroundings. The results support the manufacturer's claim.

## 5. Conclusion

The rig performs well and the experiment was successful overall.
